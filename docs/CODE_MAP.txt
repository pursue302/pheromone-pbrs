Workspace code map
==================

run.py dispatches the configurations in configs/. Generated training outputs
go under runs/. Rebuilt statistics go under analysis_results/.

01_goal_preservation/goal_diagnostic.py performs exact finite-horizon planning
and checks goal preservation against goal_diagnostic.json.

02_source_audit/run_replication.py reproduces the historical MountainCar runs.
upstream_author/, comparison_agents/ and upstream/ retain the reference sources.
feedback/ contains the earlier pheromone experiments and feature utilities.
trace_repair.py implements the repair arms; direct_reset.py calls the original
learn() after clearing the eligibility trace. repair/ and direct_reset/ hold
the corresponding run manifests.

03_controlled_study/study.py implements trajectory memory and Return-Gate.
study_v1.py is the early experiment variant. The strong and unbounded variants
control for retained history and update size. discovery/, confirm_*, strong_*
and unbounded_* contain the experiment manifests; analyze.py computes the
paired comparisons.

04_navigation_validation/experiments/engine.py runs the independent navigation
tests, including the state-only and strength controls. analyze_validation.py
computes paired statistics. Each experiment owns its plot_*.py modules.
paper/snapshot_data.py assembles the paper inputs; paper/build.py handles publication.

environments/gridworld.py and navigation.py provide grid dynamics and wrappers.
environments/historical.py and gym_legacy/ provide historical MountainCar.
policies/sarsa.py supplies action selection, value evaluation and trace updates.
policies/author_port/ and paper_author/ preserve compatible author policies.

The public paper source, figures, tables and final PDF are in paper/.
Historical hashes and archive inventories refer to the original sources.
Raw per-seed records, models and teacher priors are in the offline archive.
