The prior studies remain unchanged, including their failed held-out mixtures.
This study does not assume that a positive innovation result must exist.

A. Repair ablation: 200 complete old-Gym MountainCar episodes per run, cap
1000, goal x >= .5, environmental reward -1 at every step. Author tile coding,
alpha .5/10, gamma 1, lambda .9, epsilon .025 times .99 EVERY STEP, same seeds
and random generators as paper_static_replication_20261120. Reuse its actual
author runs as the source control. New arms: reset traces only; standard Dutch
trace plus reset; raw static PA with boundary fixes plus reset; the same with
standard Dutch trace. The static prior is the same mature historical_A bank.
The teacher costs 111259 interactions, and is held fixed, not 100 independent
teachers. All student Q values start at zero. Report author ABS and zero-based
AIBS plus independently evaluated final environmental returns. Paper Table 7
contains best-training-episode statistics, NOT these final-policy returns.

B. Equal-total-budget study: MountainCar 60000, U-detour / four-rooms /
slippery-rooms 30000 environmental interactions, caps 1000 / 200. The latter
three reuse the actual course environment; slipperiness .15. Epsilon .025
constant; corrected True Online SARSA, author action-specific features, alpha
.5/10 or .1/10, gamma 1 / .99, lambda .9, identical for ALL new-method arms.
The first 25 percent of interactions (rounded to a complete episode) use no
shaping. All methods have exactly the same warm-up actions and observations.
No external teacher or extra oracle samples are used. Q is retained after
warm-up. Original released global and local pheromone rules are raw controls.

New proposal: trajectory-quality-weighted state potential. Each successful
completed trajectory contributes once per state bin, with weight 1/length,
target its actual discounted remaining environmental return at the last visit.
Evaporate numerator and denominator together by .99; their ratio corrects
visitation frequency. Unseen target is -200. Use .1 times the estimate.
The offline memory freezes after warm-up. A separate online memory receives
only new real successful trajectories, never repeated elite paths. Static,
dynamic, fixed 50/50 mix, local count-gated mix beta(s)=.5*n(s)/(n(s)+5), and
unbounded-gate ablation are compared. Publish once per 10 completed episodes,
limit per-state potential change to 1 environmental reward unit, except the
explicit unbounded ablation. Freeze published state potential within episodes;
initial potential is actually evaluated, endpoint potential is zero. The
telescoping identity is checked on every completed trajectory. Failed or
incomplete trajectories do not become successful pheromone evidence.
An unweighted Monte Carlo control tests whether ant quality weighting helps.

Related-work transferred baselines: normalized absolute velocity, and +1
shifted velocity (MountainCar); negative Manhattan potential on grids. These
transfer heuristics into our SARSA backbone; they do not reproduce the 2025
paper's DQN, exponential potential, hyperparameters, or published task budget.

Discovery seeds 30000-30007 only. Final selection is written to SELECTION.txt
before confirmation seeds 31000+ are run. Confirm 40 MountainCar seeds and
at least 20 per grid if a discovery candidate merits it. If discovery is
negative, report it and still confirm a bounded representative candidate, not
silently abandon difficult environments. No changes after seeing holdout.
Report full methods, failures, means, success, learning-curve AUC, paired
bootstrap 95% intervals (20000 resamples, deterministic), and paired two-sided
permutation p-values with Holm correction for the declared primary comparisons.
Primary final comparisons: proposal versus static, dynamic, fixed mixture,
plain, and unweighted MC, jointly across the four scenarios. A gain over a poor
baseline alone is not a gain over ordinary SARSA. Evaluation trajectories are
never used for potential updates or hyperparameter selection.

All costs, raw per-seed results, models, source hashes and failed discovery
variants are retained. CPU wall seconds are reported separately from samples;
parallel timings on this machine cannot be ranked against paper seconds.
