Frozen before any fresh validation outcomes, 2026-11-20.

Purpose: independently validate the previously post-hoc navigation finding;
test whether removing action dependence or matching first two moments is
sufficient; check a small prespecified shaping-strength range. The selected
method and all old results remain unchanged. This is a follow-up study informed
by the earlier study, not a retroactive re-registration of its hypotheses.

Fresh training seeds 40000-40019 (20), U-detour/four-rooms/slippery-rooms.
Each method gets 30000 real interactions, including the common 25% warm-up;
cap 200, gamma .99, epsilon .025, alpha .1/10, lambda .9; identical features,
action selection, RNG, checkpoints and original-reward evaluation as the
frozen research_story_20261120/study_unbounded_controls.py engine.

Ten methods: plain; raw_static; raw_dynamic; state_raw_static;
state_raw_matched; return_static; return_gate_unbounded;
mc_dynamic_all_unbounded; gate_scale003; gate_scale030.

State-raw-static averages the released raw pheromone score over actions and
then freezes this scalar state potential. State-raw-matched applies an affine
transformation using mean and SD of the state-raw scores and calibrated static
potentials ONLY on successfully observed warm-up states. No per-state target
fitting or environment model is used; unseen states receive the same affine
map. If fewer than two observed states or a zero SD occurs, publish a constant
matching the observed calibrated mean (zero if none). Match first two moments,
not the complete potential distribution. These controls do NOT isolate every
normalization or memory component.

The original selected gate uses scale .1. Two new arms use .03 and .3 with
all other parameters unchanged. No best-scale selection after outcomes.
Every original method retains its frozen implementation; do not fix or tune
methods on these data.

Primary family: selected gate vs raw_static and raw_dynamic in all three
scenarios, six paired two-sided sign-flip tests, 20000 simulations, Holm .05.
Also report paired bootstrap 95% mean-difference intervals. Seed is the unit.
Secondary representation/strong-baseline family: gate vs state_raw_static,
state_raw_matched, return_static, MC-all-unbounded and plain, 15 tests jointly.
Sensitivity family: scale .03/.3 vs .1, six tests jointly. Report all means,
SDs, successes, original discounted return and original-return learning AUC.
Intervals are descriptive; use adjusted p-values for rejection decisions.

The goal-preservation diagnostic is separate and uses exact finite-horizon
dynamic programming on a deliberately small, fully known 5x7 grid, cap40,
gamma .99, sparse +1 goal reward, and a distracting positive feature at (4,2).
Compare no shaping, naive beta*h(next), and beta*(gamma*h(next)-h(current)),
with actual terminal/truncation potential zero, at beta in
[0,.01,.03,.05,.1,.3,1,3,10]. This verifies objective manipulation versus the
PBRS wrapper; it is not a training-performance experiment, does not charge
model backups as real samples, and is not evidence of our potential's accuracy.
